# Task 3B: MD Strategic Discussion Talking Points

## Brand Selected

**UrbanPulse (Men's Grooming)**

---

# Objective of the Discussion

The purpose of this discussion is not to review campaign metrics or weekly optimizations.

Instead, the goal is to understand how Amazon fits into UrbanPulse's broader growth strategy and identify opportunities to increase revenue, improve profitability, and strengthen competitive positioning over the next quarter.

---

# Current Situation

Based on account performance over the past month:

- Monthly Ad Spend: ₹4L
- Current ROAS: 2.1
- Performance remains below the target ROAS of 3.0
- Significant optimization work is underway including keyword restructuring, negative keyword audits, and competitive analysis
- Competitor listings appear stronger in visual presentation and product positioning

While campaign performance is below expectations today, the larger opportunity may extend beyond campaign settings alone.

---

# Key Discussion Areas

## 1. Amazon's Role in Overall Business Growth

Questions:

- What percentage of total company revenue currently comes from Amazon?
- Is Amazon expected to be a growth engine or a profitability engine?
- How important is marketplace growth compared to D2C and offline channels?
- What revenue target does the business want to achieve in the next 12 months?

Reason:

Understanding strategic priorities helps determine whether we should optimize primarily for profitability, market share, customer acquisition, or revenue growth.

---

## 2. Competitive Positioning

Observation:

Several competitors appear to have stronger product presentation, particularly in image quality, brand storytelling, and listing design.

Discussion:

- Which competitors does UrbanPulse consider the biggest threat?
- Are competitors winning primarily on price, branding, reviews, or visibility?
- What differentiation does UrbanPulse want customers to remember?

Opportunity:

Improving listing quality could increase conversion rates without requiring proportional increases in advertising spend.

---

## 3. Marketplace Expansion Opportunities

Current focus is Amazon.

Questions:

- Is the company currently active on Flipkart?
- Are there plans to expand advertising activity beyond Amazon?
- Which marketplaces are generating the strongest margins today?

Potential Opportunity:

Testing additional marketplaces could diversify acquisition channels and reduce dependency on a single platform.

---

## 4. Profitability Versus Growth

Discussion:

If additional budget becomes available, should it be invested in:

- Greater advertising reach
- New product launches
- Brand awareness campaigns
- Marketplace expansion
- Creative and listing improvements

Reason:

The correct recommendation depends on the company's current stage of growth and capital allocation priorities.

---

## 5. Customer Acquisition and Brand Building

Questions:

- Is UrbanPulse focused on maximizing immediate sales or acquiring repeat customers?
- What percentage of customers purchase more than once?
- Are there products that create stronger customer lifetime value?

Opportunity:

If repeat purchase behavior is strong, the business may be able to tolerate higher acquisition costs today in exchange for larger long-term customer value.

---

# Growth Opportunities Identified

## Opportunity 1: Conversion Rate Improvement

The strongest immediate opportunity appears to be improving product presentation.

Potential actions:

- Better lifestyle photography
- Stronger product benefits communication
- Enhanced brand storytelling
- Improved product detail pages

Expected outcome:

Higher conversion rates without requiring proportional increases in advertising expenditure.

---

## Opportunity 2: Competitive Benchmarking Program

Develop a structured review of:

- Competitor pricing
- Product positioning
- Review counts
- Visual quality
- Advertising presence

Expected outcome:

More informed strategic decisions and improved market positioning.

---

## Opportunity 3: Multi-Channel Expansion Assessment

Evaluate whether UrbanPulse should expand advertising activity into:

- Flipkart
- D2C acquisition channels
- Other ecommerce marketplaces

Expected outcome:

Reduced platform concentration risk and additional revenue opportunities.

---

# Information Required Before Final Recommendations

To provide more precise recommendations, I would want visibility into:

- Total company revenue
- Marketplace contribution by channel
- Product-level profitability
- Customer repeat purchase rates
- Category growth trends
- Competitor market share estimates
- Current expansion priorities
- Available growth budget

---

# Closing Discussion Question

If UrbanPulse successfully doubles marketplace revenue over the next 12 months, what would leadership consider the primary driver of that success?

Would it be:

- Market share gains?
- New customer acquisition?
- Improved profitability?
- New marketplace expansion?
- Brand positioning improvements?

The answer would help align advertising strategy with the broader business vision.

---

# Strategic Summary

The most important takeaway is that UrbanPulse's challenge may not be purely an advertising optimization problem.

Campaign performance reflects the combined impact of:

- Product positioning
- Listing quality
- Competitive strength
- Customer perception
- Marketplace strategy

Addressing these factors together creates a stronger path to sustainable growth than focusing exclusively on ROAS improvement.
