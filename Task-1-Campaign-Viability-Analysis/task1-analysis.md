# Task 1: Campaign Viability Analysis

## Brand Overview

**Brand:** GlowNest  
**Product:** Premium Face Serum

### Given Data

| Metric | Value |
|----------|----------|
| Selling Price (SP) | ₹800 |
| Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) | ₹280 |
| Amazon Referral Fee | 12% |
| Monthly Ad Spend | ₹3,00,000 |
| Units Sold via Ads | 1,500 |
| Organic Units Sold | 2,000 |
| Fixed Costs | ₹1,50,000 |

---

# Q1. Unit Economics

## Step 1: Gross Margin per Unit

Formula:

Gross Margin = Selling Price − COGS

Calculation:

= ₹800 − ₹280

= ₹520

### Gross Margin

**₹520 per unit**

---

## Step 2: Amazon Fee per Unit

Formula:

Amazon Fee = SP × Referral Fee %

Calculation:

= ₹800 × 12%

= ₹96

### Amazon Fee

**₹96 per unit**

---

## Step 3: Advertising Cost per Unit

Formula:

Ad Cost per Unit = Ad Spend ÷ Ad Units Sold

Calculation:

= ₹3,00,000 ÷ 1,500

= ₹200

### Advertising Cost per Unit

**₹200 per unit**

---

## Step 4: Net Margin per Ad-Attributed Unit

Formula:

Net Margin = Gross Margin − Amazon Fee − Ad Cost

Calculation:

= ₹520 − ₹96 − ₹200

= ₹224

### Net Margin

**₹224 per ad-attributed unit**

---

# Q2. Monthly Profitability

## Profit from Ad-Attributed Sales

Ad Unit Profit = Net Margin × Ad Units

= ₹224 × 1,500

= ₹3,36,000

---

## Profit from Organic Sales

Organic Net Margin per Unit

= ₹520 − ₹96

= ₹424

Organic Profit

= ₹424 × 2,000

= ₹8,48,000

---

## Total Contribution Before Fixed Costs

= ₹3,36,000 + ₹8,48,000

= ₹11,84,000

---

## Monthly Profit

Formula:

Monthly Profit = Total Contribution − Fixed Costs

= ₹11,84,000 − ₹1,50,000

= ₹10,34,000

### Monthly Profitability

**₹10,34,000 profit per month**

---

# Q3. ACOS and TACOS Analysis

## ACOS Calculation

Ad Revenue

= ₹800 × 1,500

= ₹12,00,000

Formula:

ACOS = Ad Spend ÷ Ad Revenue × 100

Calculation:

= ₹3,00,000 ÷ ₹12,00,000 × 100

= 25%

### ACOS

**25%**

---

## TACOS Calculation

Total Revenue

= ₹800 × (1,500 + 2,000)

= ₹28,00,000

Formula:

TACOS = Ad Spend ÷ Total Revenue × 100

Calculation:

= ₹3,00,000 ÷ ₹28,00,000 × 100

= 10.71%

### TACOS

**10.71%**

---

## Interpretation

The current advertising performance appears healthy.

Reasons:

- ACOS of 25% indicates that only ₹25 is spent on advertising for every ₹100 generated through ads.
- TACOS of 10.71% indicates that advertising consumes a relatively small percentage of total business revenue.
- Organic sales account for more than half of total units sold, reducing dependence on paid advertising.
- The business remains strongly profitable after considering advertising costs and fixed expenses.

Overall, GlowNest demonstrates a healthy balance between paid acquisition and organic demand generation.

---

# Q4. Break-Even Ad Spend

The objective is to determine the maximum advertising spend the business can sustain before profit becomes zero.

## Contribution Margin Without Advertising

Per Unit Margin Before Advertising

= ₹520 − ₹96

= ₹424

Total Units Sold

= 1,500 + 2,000

= 3,500

Total Contribution

= ₹424 × 3,500

= ₹14,84,000

---

## Break-Even Calculation

Break-Even Ad Spend

= Total Contribution − Fixed Costs

= ₹14,84,000 − ₹1,50,000

= ₹13,34,000

### Maximum Affordable Ad Spend

**₹13,34,000 per month**

---

## Corresponding ACOS

Ad Revenue remains:

₹12,00,000

Formula:

ACOS = ₹13,34,000 ÷ ₹12,00,000 × 100

= 111.17%

### Break-Even ACOS

**111.17%**

---

## Interpretation

Although the business mathematically breaks even at this spend level, an ACOS above 100% means advertising costs exceed advertising-generated revenue.

This is operationally unacceptable.

In practice, GlowNest should maintain ACOS significantly below break-even levels to preserve profitability and cash flow.

# Q5. Scenario Analysis: ACOS Increases to 40%

## New Conditions

| Metric | Value |
|----------|----------|
| ACOS | 40% |
| Ad Units Sold | 1,200 |
| Organic Units | 2,000 |

---

## New Ad Revenue

= ₹800 × 1,200

= ₹9,60,000

---

## New Ad Spend

= 40% × ₹9,60,000

= ₹3,84,000

---

## New Ad Cost Per Unit

= ₹3,84,000 ÷ 1,200

= ₹320

---

## New Net Margin Per Ad Unit

= ₹520 − ₹96 − ₹320

= ₹104

---

## New Ad Profit

= ₹104 × 1,200

= ₹1,24,800

---

## Organic Profit

= ₹424 × 2,000

= ₹8,48,000

---

## Total Monthly Profit

= ₹1,24,800 + ₹8,48,000 − ₹1,50,000

= ₹8,22,800

### Monthly Profit

**₹8,22,800**

---

## Impact Assessment

Profit remains positive but decreases substantially.

Comparison:

| Scenario | Profit |
|----------|----------|
| Current | ₹10,34,000 |
| ACOS 40% | ₹8,22,800 |

Reduction:

₹2,11,200

---

## Recommendation

Rather than immediately reducing ad spend, I would investigate the root causes behind the efficiency decline.

Potential causes:

- Increased keyword competition
- Weak product listing quality
- Declining conversion rates
- Poor audience targeting
- Rising CPCs

Optimization should focus on improving conversion efficiency before reducing growth investment.

---

# Q6. Scenario Analysis: Doubling Ad Spend

## New Conditions

| Metric | Value |
|----------|----------|
| Ad Spend | ₹6,00,000 |
| Ad Units | 2,500 |

---

## Ad Revenue

= ₹800 × 2,500

= ₹20,00,000

---

## ACOS

= ₹6,00,000 ÷ ₹20,00,000 × 100

= 30%

### New ACOS

**30%**

---

## Ad Cost Per Unit

= ₹6,00,000 ÷ 2,500

= ₹240

---

## Net Margin Per Ad Unit

= ₹520 − ₹96 − ₹240

= ₹184

---

## Ad Profit

= ₹184 × 2,500

= ₹4,60,000

---

## Organic Profit

= ₹424 × 2,000

= ₹8,48,000

---

## Total Monthly Profit

= ₹4,60,000 + ₹8,48,000 − ₹1,50,000

= ₹11,58,000

### Monthly Profit

**₹11,58,000**

---

# Final Recommendation

Comparison:

| Scenario | Monthly Profit |
|----------|----------|
| Current | ₹10,34,000 |
| Scale Up | ₹11,58,000 |

Profit increases by:

₹1,24,000

The scaling decision appears economically attractive because:

- Profitability improves
- ACOS remains within a manageable range
- Revenue growth exceeds additional advertising costs

However, evidence of diminishing returns is already visible because sales growth is not proportional to spending growth.

I would recommend scaling gradually while monitoring:

- ACOS
- TACOS
- Conversion Rate
- Incremental Profit
- Organic Revenue Growth

This approach balances growth with profitability and reduces the risk of inefficient advertising expenditure.

---

# Key Business Insight

The most important observation is that GlowNest's profitability is driven not only by advertising efficiency but also by strong organic sales.

The low TACOS and high organic contribution indicate that advertising is supporting broader brand visibility rather than functioning as the sole source of demand. This creates a more sustainable and resilient ecommerce business model.
